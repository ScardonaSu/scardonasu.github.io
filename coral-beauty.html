01 / CONTEXT & ROLE

One experience, from identity to interaction.

Coral Beauty Studio is my sister’s beauty business in Envigado. I developed the brand identity and logo, wrote the website copy, designed the interface and implemented the website in React.

The design goal was to introduce the studio, make its services easy to explore and provide a clear route to an appointment enquiry through WhatsApp. The appointment is confirmed by the studio team.

This case documents the current local website and newly created wireframes. The Figma Make prototype and the current wireframe documentation are separate artifacts; the wireframes are not presented as original pre-development evidence.

02 / VISUAL DIRECTION

A calm, recognizable visual language.

The identity combines deep green, warm ivory and a gold accent. Cormorant Garamond gives the headings and wordmark an editorial character, while Montserrat supports navigation, descriptions and actions.

The hero pairs a short headline with a service description and a primary appointment enquiry action. Its editorial image was generated with AI as a visual resource; it does not document a client or treatment outcome.

The page moves from presentation to services, results, gallery, the studio story, testimonials and contact. The wireframe makes that hierarchy visible before discussing colors and imagery.

04 / STRUCTURE & INTERFACE

From grayscale structure to the implemented design.

The editable Figma wireframes describe content groups, service cards, image placeholders and calls to action. They use a neutral typeface and no photographs, separating layout decisions from the finished visual identity.

Adapt the hierarchy to a smaller screen.

On mobile, the content stacks vertically and the implementation keeps an appointment enquiry action within reach. The wireframe is a structural proposal: its placeholder order is not a pixel-perfect copy of the existing hero.

05 / DESIGN CONNECTED TO CODE

The interface, implemented in React.

A component-based page

The local project uses React, TypeScript and Vite. Services, gallery, testimonials and contact are organized in separate components; a language context supports Spanish and English.

A clear appointment handoff

The primary action leads to WhatsApp for an availability enquiry. This case shows the interface only: no appointment was submitted and no automated booking or payment is claimed.

06 / CURRENT STATUS & NEXT STEPS

Ready to review, before a public launch.

The current evidence is a working local implementation, a Figma Make prototype and editable desktop and mobile wireframes. A public website link will be added once the studio content is approved and the site is deployed.

Before launch, confirm business figures, testimonials, promotions and service claims with the owner. Replace stock result comparisons with authorized photographs of the same real treatment.

Next, evaluate whether people can identify a suitable service, find the location and understand how to request an appointment. No usability study, conversion improvement or business result is claimed in this first version.
