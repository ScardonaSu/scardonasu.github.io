01 / THE CHALLENGE

Make a new product easy to understand.

KŪBIK is my smoothie venture, developed from Envigado. The digital experience introduces three blends and lets people build a selection before confirming a request through WhatsApp.

The design challenge was to balance an expressive brand with practical buying information: what each blend contains, how the varieties differ, how much a pack costs, and what happens after adding it.

I directed the visual and product decisions and iterated on the frontend with AI assistance for design exploration, implementation and review. This case describes that collaborative workflow; it does not imply unaided authorship of every line of code.

Evidence in this first version comes from the live website. Figma screens and their place in the design timeline will be documented when the file is available. No user-research study or conversion uplift is claimed.

03 / BRAND & INTERFACE

Expressive where it matters. Clear where it counts.

Cream, plum and lime build a recognizable visual world. A large sans-serif headline and contrasting italic serif give the hero personality, while the primary action leads directly to the blends. The cup and packaging illustrations are conceptual assets.

04 / PRODUCT INFORMATION

Three blends, distinct choices.

Verde Vital, Rojo Silvestre and Sol Tropical use distinct colors, ingredient labels and sensory descriptions. The live catalogue shows 120 g packs and provisional COP prices. This makes the offer more concrete without presenting availability or pricing as finalized.

05 / CART INTERACTION

Keep the selection within reach.

The side panel keeps the selected products, quantity controls and estimated subtotal together. Adding a pack and adjusting the quantity were checked on the live site. Selection persistence was observed during the earlier website review.

06 / REQUEST, NOT ONLINE CHECKOUT

Make the next step explicit.

The review step asks for delivery details and consent before preparing a request. The interface describes a private order record and WhatsApp communication. This review stopped before entering personal information or submitting anything; backend delivery was not tested.

07 / RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Carry the same identity into mobile.

The mobile layout retains the type contrast, brand colors and direct route to the catalogue. The live interface was reviewed at 390 px wide without horizontal overflow. This is a layout check, not a usability study.

08 / IMPLEMENTATION & LEARNING

A live interface. An evolving venture.

Design connected to code

The project uses Astro and frontend code, with deployment on Render. It combines an expressive landing page, product information, a selection panel and an editorial journal. Spanish and English storefronts are available; the journal is identified as Spanish content.

What remains to validate

Confirm the commercial offer, availability and delivery conditions; review consistency in the data-use explanations; test the complete request path with controlled test data; and evaluate how easily people compare blends and understand the next step.

Current functionality is evidence of implementation, not evidence of sales, customer satisfaction or conversion improvement. Product imagery is conceptual and the commercial offer remains in validation.
