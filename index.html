02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

A designer’s eye.

I work where visual design and frontend development meet, turning an idea into an interface that a team can understand and build.

My background includes Angular and WordPress development, alongside UX/UI design and visual post-production. That experience shapes how I think about layouts, interactions, and the details that connect a mockup to its implementation.

For Colombia Eléctrica, I designed the game’s interface in Figma and prepared screen-by-screen mockups for the development team.
